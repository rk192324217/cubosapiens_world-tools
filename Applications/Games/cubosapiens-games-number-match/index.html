HOW TO PLAY
  • Match numbers that are equal (e.g., 3 and 3) or add up to 10 (e.g., 4 and 6, 1 and 9).
  • Pairs can be adjacent horizontally, vertically, diagonally, or wrapping from the end of a line to the start of the next.
  • Matched cells in between are skipped when checking adjacency.
  • Tap ADD NUMBERS to duplicate remaining numbers to the bottom when out of moves.
