How to Play

  • Select a difficulty to start the race.
  • Type the text as fast and accurately as you can.
  • Every correct keystroke accelerates your car.
  • Every mistake slows your car down!
  • No Backspace Allowed: If you make a mistake, you must type the correct character to continue.
  • Solo Race: Race against your last run, or a preset pace car if it's your first time.
  • Player VS AI: Race against a preloaded AI opponent with realistic, human-like pacing.
