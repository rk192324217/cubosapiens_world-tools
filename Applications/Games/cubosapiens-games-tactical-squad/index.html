How to Play

  • 🪖 Click a player unit to select it.
  • ⬜ Click an adjacent empty cell to move.
  • 👾 Click an adjacent enemy to attack.
  • 🪨 Obstacles cannot be crossed.
  • 🏆 Defeat all enemies to win.
  • ⌨️ Press R to restart.
  • 🌗 Press T to toggle theme.
  • ⬅️➡️ Use Arrow Keys to select soldiers.
  • ♿ Keyboard shortcuts improve accessibility.
