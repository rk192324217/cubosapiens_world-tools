How to Play

Objective

Clear the board without clicking on any hidden mines. Use flags to mark suspected mine locations.

Controls

  • Left Click / Tap: Reveal a cell.
  • Right Click / Long Press: Toggle flag (🚩) on a cell.
  • Double Click: Chord (if surrounding flags match the cell's number, all other adjacent unflagged cells are revealed).
  • Hovering: Hovering over a revealed number highlights its unrevealed neighbors to help you plan chords.

Replay System

After a game ends (win or lose), click the REPLAY button in the game over modal to watch your entire play sequence step-by-step in real-time!
