Captain's Operations Manual

Welcome, Commander. You have been granted a basic starship and a 10,000 credit loan. Your objective is simple: trade, build, and pay off your debt to become a Trading Tycoon.

1. The Trading Loop

  • Buy commodities cheap where supply is high, warp to other systems, and sell them high.
  • Commodity values fluctuate based on location demands and unexpected market shocks.

2. Debt and Banking

  • You owe 5,000 Credits to the Syndicate. They charge a steep 10% interest rate per day. Pay this off quickly!
  • Store extra credits in the Galactic Credit Union to earn 3% interest per day. Banks also protect your money from pirates.

3. Ship Upgrades

  • Cargo expansion: Carry more units of commodities to scale your gains.
  • Deflector Shields: Safeguards your ship's hull from cosmic hazards and pirate damage.
  • Hyperdrive engines: Lowers the chance of intercepting pirate fleets.
  • Keep your hull repaired. If your ship's hull integrity hits 0%, it is game over.

4. Warp and Events

  • Traveling between planets takes exactly 1 day.
  • During warp, you may encounter solar flares, space debris, or Space Pirates!
  • If pirates intercept you, you can try to Flee, Pay Bribe, or Fight. Fight success rates depend on your deflector shields.
