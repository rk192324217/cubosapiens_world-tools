# Trading Empire Game

A browser-based, standalone cyberpunk cargo trading simulation. Travel between distant ports, buy low, sell high, manage your bank and syndicate loans, upgrade your spaceship, and avoid cosmic threats!

## Project Structure

- `index.html` - The markup and layout of the game.
- `style.css` - Beautiful cyberpunk themed CSS styling with full support for Light/Dark modes and responsiveness.
- `script.js` - Dynamic game logic, market price calculations, sound synthesizer (Web Audio API), and game saving (localStorage).

## How to Play

1. Start with 10,000 Credits and a 5,000 Credit debt to the syndicate loan shark.
2. Trade five types of commodities across five planetary ports, capitalizing on price variations and random market events.
3. Manage your money by paying off your loan (debt grows at 10% daily) or depositing credits into the Galactic Bank (earns 3% daily).
4. Upgrade your ship's cargo bay, shields, and warp drive in the shipyard to improve trading efficiency and survive pirate attacks.
5. Travel to new planets to advance the day counter. You have 30 days to build your net worth, pay off all debt, and secure your place as a Trading Tycoon!
